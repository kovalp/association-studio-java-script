Association studio

Calculate the association score between two bounding boxes.

Three metrics can be compared:

  • GIoU: generalized intersection over union
  • Maha: Gaussian of the Mahalanobis-distance D with static covariance
  • SMMa: exp(-D) with size-modulated diagonal covariance.
